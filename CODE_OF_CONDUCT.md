# Code of Conduct

dirgo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience level or background.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or private.
- Don't publish other people's private information.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@mohsinkaleem](https://github.com/mohsinkaleem). If it's something you'd rather not discuss in public, open an issue asking for a private contact and leave the details out; you'll be contacted directly.

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits, or other contributions that break this code of conduct, and may block people who repeatedly or seriously break it.
